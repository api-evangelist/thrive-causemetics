Celebrating Every Mom: A Mother's Day Giving Event with Mary's Place Moms are superheroes, and deserve to feel celebrated and appreciated everyday. This Mother's Day, we did just that by teaming up with Mary’s Place to provide 75 unhoused moms with a morning of connection and care. At the Allen Family Center in Seattle, one of Mary's Place shelter locations, we distributed nearly $60,000 worth of clean makeup and skincare products to thank these mothers for all that they do.
