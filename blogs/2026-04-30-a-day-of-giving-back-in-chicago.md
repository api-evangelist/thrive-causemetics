A Day of Giving Back in Chicago Giving is at the heart of everything we do, and on March 19, 2026, that mission came to life in a powerful way in Chicago. We partnered with longtime friend of the brand and creator Madeline Ford to bring together a community of changemakers for a meaningful day of service. Alongside Team Thrive Causemetics, Madeline and a group of creator friends spent the day volunteering with one of our National Giving Partners, Feeding America , at their local network partner, the Greater Chicago Food Depository .
