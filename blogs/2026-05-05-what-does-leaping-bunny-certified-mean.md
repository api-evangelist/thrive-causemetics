Cruelty-free beauty has become a priority for many shoppers, but the language around ethical production can feel surprisingly murky. A quick glance at packaging might reveal phrases like “not tested on animals” or “cruelty-free,” but those claims aren’t always regulated or verified. Without a clear standard, it can be difficult to tell which products truly meet strict no-animal-testing expectations.
